## Accuracy check (passed)
- Single-electron buildup of an interference pattern was demonstrated and filmed by Tonomura's team at Hitachi (published 1989). They used an electron biprism, which splits each electron's wave into two paths, the electron equivalent of two slits. The on-screen label says so.
- Gaining which-path information destroys the interference pattern. This is standard quantum mechanics, confirmed experimentally many times.
- The S6 ripple visual is a teaching illustration of the wave description, labeled as an illustration in the caption source line.
