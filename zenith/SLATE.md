# Zenith Episode Slate — Season 1
Each episode is one subject, understood all the way down (about 3 minutes, a chain of "but *why*?"). ⭐ = recommended for the first release batch. Every episode maps to a Frontier Path major so viewers always have a next step.

**Three horizons in every episode:** close the lesson with **Achieved** (what's proven, with sources) → **Reaching** (what labs are working on right now, named) → **Unlocks** (what it could enable, always phrased as "could" or "if", never as fact). Subjects with a big frontier also get a dedicated **Frontier episode** (🚀), listed at the end of each sector below.
